Presets

Start with a look. Finish it yourself.

A preset in VETRA is only slider settings, so nothing is hidden: apply one, see exactly what it changed, and keep adjusting. Amount takes it from 0%, none of it, through 100%, the look as designed, to 200%.

  • 116 presets across Film, Cinematic, Landscape, Portrait, Black & White and seven more categories
  • Copy a look from any picture: a favourite photo, a still from a film, a screenshot
  • Save your own, mark favourites and search them all

Why VETRA

Your photos are yours. The editor should act like it.

{{icon:layers}}

Originals are never changed

Edits save themselves as you go, in a small .vetra.json file next to each photo. Remove that file and the photo is exactly as it was.

{{icon:lock}}

Nothing leaves your computer

No account, no upload, no usage tracking. The one thing VETRA downloads is the optional AI models, and it asks before it does.

{{icon:history}}

Every step can be taken back

History lists each change to a photo in order. Click a step to return to it, select several to remove them, or undo with Ctrl Z.

Local adjustments

Edit one part of the picture.

Brighten a face, deepen a sky, calm a background. Each mask carries its own exposure, colour and detail settings, and masks can be added together, subtracted or inverted.

  • Sky, Subject and Background are found for you, by AI that runs on your own machine
  • Radial, Linear and Brush for shapes you draw
  • Luminance and Colour to select by brightness or by a colour you click
  • Lens blur with a depth map, a focus range and click-to-focus

Develop

The controls you expect, where you expect them.

White balance, tone and presence at the top; curve, colour mixer, grading, detail and effects below. Double-click any slider to reset it, click its number to type a value.

  • Tone: exposure, contrast, highlights, shadows, whites, blacks
  • Presence: texture, clarity, dehaze, vibrance, saturation
  • Crop, straighten, rotate and flip, with fixed aspect ratios
  • Spot removal: heal, clone or remove

Export and backups

Out the door, and safe at home.

Export one photo or a whole album. The export runs in the background while you keep editing, and it never overwrites a file that is already there.

  • JPEG, PNG or TIFF, at the original size or resized by the long edge
  • Several places at once: a USB stick, a synced folder, next to the original
  • Backups of your edits, albums, stars and presets to folders you choose, kept up to date as you work
  • Restore after a reinstall or on a new computer

Questions

Asked before downloading.

Is VETRA really free?

Yes. The {{version}} test version is free to download and use, with no account and no payment details. It is a test version, so expect some rough edges, and please report what you find.

Will it change or move my photos?

No. VETRA never writes to your original files. Your edits are stored in a small .vetra.json file beside each photo, and exports are always new files. Photos stay in the folders they are already in.

Are my photos uploaded anywhere?

No. Everything, including the AI masks and lens blur, runs on your own computer. VETRA has no account system and sends no usage data. The only downloads it makes are the optional AI models, about 320 MB in all, and it asks first. Read the privacy page.

Windows says "Windows protected your PC". Is that normal?

For this test version, yes. The installer is not code-signed yet, so Windows SmartScreen does not recognise it. Click More info, then Run anyway. You can confirm the file is the genuine one by checking its SHA-256 checksum on the download page.

Which photo formats does it open?

JPEG, PNG, TIFF, WebP and BMP, plus RAW files from most cameras: CR2, CR3, NEF, ARW, RAF, ORF, RW2, DNG, PEF, SRW, X3F and others. HEIC photos from iPhones are not supported yet; set the iPhone camera to "Most Compatible" to get JPEGs.

Do I need a powerful computer?

No graphics card is needed. On a six-core PC without one, finding a subject or building a depth map for lens blur takes around ten seconds, and exporting eleven photos of up to 13 megapixels took 35 seconds.

Is there a version for Mac?

Not at the moment. VETRA is available for Windows 10 and 11.

How do I remove it?

Windows Settings › Apps › VETRA › Uninstall. It asks before deleting your library and settings. Your photos, and the edit files next to them, are never touched.
