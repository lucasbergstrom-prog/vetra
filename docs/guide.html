Guide

From install to first export.

Ten minutes of reading. The guided tour inside VETRA covers the same ground hands-on.

  • Install

  • Edit

1. Install

Download the installer from the download page and run it. It installs for your user only, so it does not ask for admin rights. If Windows shows "Windows protected your PC", click More info and then Run anyway; the test version is not code-signed yet.

The first time VETRA opens it offers a guided tour. It is worth taking: it walks through the whole app on practice photos, lights up the part of the window each step is about, and waits for you to do it. "Show me" does any step for you. When the tour ends, the practice photos and everything done to them are removed. You can take it again from the ? menu at the top right.

2. Bring photos in

Use Import at the top left.

  • Import a folder (Ctrl O) adds every photo in a folder and, if you like, its subfolders, and can make an album named after it.
  • Add photos lets you hand-pick files from anywhere. They join the library, and the album that is open if there is one.
  • Open a single photo takes one photo straight into Develop.

You can also right-click a photo in Windows and choose Open with › VETRA. In every case the photos stay where they are.

3. Choose the keepers

Press C for Cull. One photo fills the window. Press P to pick it, X to reject it, or 1 to 5 to rate it, and VETRA moves to the next. Ctrl Z takes back the last judgement. When you are done, Add picks to album gathers the keepers.

4. Edit

Press D for Develop, or Enter on a selected photo.

  • Try a preset first. Open a category on the left, hover a preset to preview it and click to apply. Drag Amount to make it weaker or stronger.
  • Then adjust. The Basic section on the right has white balance, exposure and the other tone sliders. Double-click a slider to reset it.
  • Crop with R. Drag the handles, press Enter to apply or Esc to cancel.
  • Fix one area with a mask from the row under the histogram: Subject, Sky, Brush and the rest.
  • Check your work. Hold \ to see the original. Press J to see clipping.

There is no Save button. Every change is saved as you make it, and History, the column beside the photo, lists them all. Click any step to go back.

5. Export

Press Ctrl E, or use the Export button at the top right. It exports the selected photos, or the whole album when nothing is selected. Choose JPEG, PNG or TIFF, a size, and one or more places to save to. The first time, exports go to Pictures\VETRA Exports. The export runs in the background; click the green pill when it finishes to open the folder.

Where things are kept

WhatWhere
Your photosWherever they already were. VETRA never moves or changes them.
EditsA .vetra.json file beside each edited photo. Keep it with the photo and the edit travels too.
Library and settings%USERPROFILE%\.vetra: albums, stars and picks, your presets and preferences.
ExportsPictures\VETRA Exports until you choose other places.
Logs%USERPROFILE%\.vetra\logs, opened from the ? menu.
BackupsAny folders you add under Settings, the sliders button at the top right.
